# Disclaimer

**Gym Coach is not medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a qualified professional.**

This software generates general fitness and nutrition information. It is not a
doctor, a physiotherapist, a registered dietitian, or a certified strength coach,
and nothing it produces should be treated as a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or a
prescription.

## Before you start

Consult a physician before beginning any exercise or nutrition program,
particularly if you:

- have a diagnosed cardiac, metabolic, respiratory, or musculoskeletal condition
- are pregnant, or recently postpartum
- are under 18
- are returning from an injury, surgery, or an extended period of inactivity
- take medication that affects heart rate, blood pressure, or blood sugar

## Stop and seek medical attention

Stop training immediately and seek medical attention for chest pain, shortness of
breath disproportionate to effort, fainting or near-fainting, repeated dizziness,
or a sudden severe headache during exertion.

## What this software cannot do

**It cannot see you move.** It has no way to assess your technique, your joint
positions, your range of motion, or whether a load is safe for you today. Written
cues and conservative loading are the only tools it has. Poor technique under load
causes injury, and this software will not catch it.

It also cannot examine you, order imaging, take your history, or know anything
about you that you have not typed into it.

## Injury and pain

Gym Coach records what you report about pain and escalates patterns that warrant
professional attention. It will never name a condition or offer a diagnosis. If
something hurts, the answer is a clinician, not a chatbot.

## Your data

Gym Coach stores your training log, plans, and body metrics in **your own Notion
workspace**, under your own Notion account. The maintainers of this repository
never see it. Your data is subject to Notion's terms and privacy policy, and to
the terms of whichever Claude product you run the plugin in.
