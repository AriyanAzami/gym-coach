# Athlete profile — template

Filled by `/gym-coach:setup` from the onboarding interview. Lives as a section of the
**Coach Operating Manual** page in Notion, not as a separate file.

Every field below must be present. `Not asked` and `Declined to say` are valid values;
a blank is not — a blank is indistinguishable from "we forgot", and the next block
will guess.
